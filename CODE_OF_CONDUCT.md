# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are
not welcome in any project space. We follow the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Report misconduct to <info@chrisdaggas.com>. Reports are confidential.
The maintainer may remove comments, lock discussions or block participants.
